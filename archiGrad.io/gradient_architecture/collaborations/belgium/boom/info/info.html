BOOM

BOOM is a tree-like garden structure that combines a water basin and a flower pot into one grown object. Placed in a patio at a height of 3.2 to 4 meters, the structure collects and distributes water, offers shelter for insects and birds, and carries an embedded sensor system. Its form is generated through particle-based growth simulations in Houdini and fabricated as a physical model. • archigrad.io



01 // Inspiration

visual references from the pingo craters in Svalbard: collapsed ice-cored mounds that leave layered, eroded basins in the landscape.

03 // Water basin and flower pot

the basin holds pebbles and stones as habitat for insects, with a bathing zone of at least 2 cm depth for birds. overflow drains to surrounding planting. additions for shade include wood, water plants and a hedgehog compost bin. neighbouring trees can grow towards the structure and connect to it over time.
