DEX 2024-25 workshop Hybrid investigates the reusing, adapting, and blending of spatial and archetypical elements/environments/materials, objects/symbols from diverse backgrounds, styles, formats, and functionalities to foster new collaborations that can co-exist in the 'post-natural' era we live in. (...) where the amalgamations of nature and artifice/the biosphere and technosphere merge into one new hybrid body. The curator of DEX invites guest instructors to explore the concept of Hybrid(s), where students can create tangible/performative outcomes inspired by new media ecologies, artifacts of living on a damaged planet, the integration of organic and synthetic materials, through blending of diverse mediums and workflows.
