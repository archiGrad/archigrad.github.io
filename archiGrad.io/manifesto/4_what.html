This is the archiGrad.io platform. Our own 2.5D project visualizer and organizer.

You can think of it as a folder structure / directory that contains organized projects.


example:

commissioned_projects > projects_2026 > project_A > drawings > ... > projects_2025 > models > context_models > detail_models > model_1 > model_2 > model_3


This is our actual working directory. When we update a project, this platform changes too.
Of course it is partly curated. There are things that you can't see...



This is our internal tool for presentations, visualization, categorization and organization, and we hope you like it too.
