Ui


There are 4 ways of navigating on desktop:



- breadcrumbs → clickable directory links at the top of the page
- arrows → dive in and out of folders step by step
- movable menu → an expandable/collapsible tree where everything is visible and clickable
- 3D navigation UI on the stacks



There are 3 ways on mobile:



- arrows → dive in and out of folders step by step
- movable menu → an expandable/collapsible tree where everything is visible and clickable
- 3D navigation UI on the stacks
