Foresta Ignitia

After Spain's devastating wild fires took place this past summer in the North West part of the country, the research examines the region's relationship to energy production and studies the forest as it's own network of energy potential and production. The beautiful photos of the burned sites are taken in El Bierzo by Sara Cuerdo @saracuerdo Research: Directed by Déborah López @deborahlopezlobato and Hadin Charbel @hadincharbel with Lia Penela @liapenela Graphic Producction: Déborah López and Hadin Charbel w/ Panagiota Grivea @io_archdesigns , Sara Kemali @sarahslk_ Chanunchida Phonoi @breados.doc Mateo Rossi @mateo_rossirolando Video Production: Joris Putteners @jorisputteneers Interviews conducted for the research: Flor Álvarez Taboada, Leonor Calvo Galván, Javier Madrigal, Jose Torero, Stamatis Zografos.
