2025 Design + Build

Project Overview

Course: Design-Construction for Communities

Semester: Spring 2025

Location: Khao Yai National Park, Thailand

Duration: 6 weeks (10 June – 18 July)

Brief

Design and fabricate a 1:1 scale wayfinding signage system for Khao Yai National Park. Integrate ecological research, environmental data, and site-specific design to create an intuitive, durable, and contextually sensitive solution for park visitors.







Resources

Software: Rhinoceros 7/8, Houdini 20.5+, Blender, GIS, Python 3.12

Materials: Timber, Steel, recycled metal, biodegradable composites, Concrete

Budget: 10,000–20,000 THB per group
